🔐 Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the Online Attendance Recorder App.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: We collect your name, email address, date of birth, and login credentials during registration.
  • Attendance Records: We store attendance data associated with each student, including class, batch, and dates.
  • Device Information: We may collect IP address, browser type, and access times for analytics and security purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To manage user accounts (Admin and Student)
  • To maintain accurate attendance records
  • To enhance security and prevent unauthorized access
  • To allow password recovery via email and date of birth verification

3. Data Sharing and Security

  • We do not share your personal information with third parties.
  • Your data is stored securely and accessible only to authorized personnel (Admins).
  • We implement standard security protocols to protect against data loss or misuse.

4. User Rights

  • Users can update or delete their accounts by contacting the Admin.
  • Students can reset their password securely using DOB and email.

5. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
