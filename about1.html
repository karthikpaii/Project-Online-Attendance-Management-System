📱 About the App – Online Attendance Recorder

The Online Attendance Recorder App is a smart attendance management solution designed for schools, colleges, and other educational institutions. It provides a digital alternative to traditional paper-based attendance systems, making the process faster, more accurate, and easier to manage.

🔧 What the App Does:

  • Add, view, update, or delete student records
  • Mark daily attendance by class, batch, and date
  • Track absent and present students quickly
  • Manage user roles with a secure login system

Students can:

  • Log in to check attendance
  • Reset passwords using verified email and date of birth

💡 Key Highlights:

  • 🔐 Secure login for both admins and students
  • 📊 Real-time attendance tracking
  • 🔄 Smooth dynamic interface using AJAX
  • 📥 Responsive design for all devices
  • 🔄 Password recovery with validation

🎯 Why This App?

Managing attendance manually using books/paper is time-consuming and error-prone. This app eliminates those problems by offering an easy-to-use digital system that saves time, reduces mistakes, and keeps records organized.

Whether you're an administrator keeping track of hundreds of students, or a student wanting to check your attendance status, this app makes the process simple, secure, and efficient.
